# The manual queues of the promotion ladder, in ten facts

## Sections

- three-queues: Where a person stands on the ladder, one fact per slide.

## About

The deck is the visual of [manual-queues](../../../drs/flow/manual-queues.md), the flow FDR on the three places a commit waits for a person:
authoring, the main-UAT signature and the release-uat signature, and the one place that never waits, the slot switch. Every slide is one
fact: a proposition that is true in this repository, checkable against the record it cites, and worth one of the ten. Each slide shows a
low-res crop of the diagram the fact comes from, the ladder of
[sync-point-rules-are-drs-only](../../design/which-sync-point-rules-have-a-gate.md) and the queues of
[manual-queues-slides](../manual-queues-slides.md). Three of the ten are allowed to be funny; none is allowed to be wrong.
