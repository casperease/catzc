> **Experimental.** This article is one rule: [FDR-CD-QUEUE-SLOTSWITCHISAUTOMATED](../../drs/flow/manual-queues.md#rule-fdr-cd-queue-slotswitchisautomated) and [FDR-CD-PRODAPPROVALGATE](../../drs/flow/cd-discipline-and-promotion-flow.md#rule-fdr-cd-prodapprovalgate) merged into one. It holds nothing beyond what those two rules say.

Two rules separate who acts from what is automated. The slot switch that makes the pre-prod occupant the live production occupant is a monitored, fully automated procedure: no person approves it or triggers it by hand, and it reverses by the same automated path. A person is instead asked at the approval gate every prod-tier environment carries, an Approval check by one named approver group, declared in `environment-approvals.yml` and applied by `Sync-AdoEnvironmentApprovals`, never set in a portal.
