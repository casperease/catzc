# Two records, one fact, in fourteen pairs

## Sections

- truth-needs-one-version: Every slide joins two decision records at the seam where one needs the other.

## About

The deck is the visual of the seams between decision records: each slide states one fact that holds only because two records hold together, and names both by code. The records are read in the [index](../../../drs/index.md). Each slide shows one diagram of the catalogue, reduced.
