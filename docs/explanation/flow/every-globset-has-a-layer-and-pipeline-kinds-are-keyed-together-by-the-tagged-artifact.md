> **Experimental.** This article is one rule: [FDR-CD-GLOBS-EVERYSETHASLAYER](../../drs/flow/durable-sha-globs.md#rule-fdr-cd-globs-everysethaslayer) and [FDR-CD-SEPARATEPIPELINEKINDS](../../drs/flow/cd-discipline-and-promotion-flow.md#rule-fdr-cd-separatepipelinekinds) merged into one. It holds nothing beyond what those two rules say.

Every globset carries a layer: `deployable-unit` for a configurable unit that ships, `loose-fileset` for a cross-cutting check surface that must earn its place by a demonstrated use such as a pipeline it triggers, and the derived-only `module`. CI, CD, CDe and DEPLOY are likewise separate pipeline kinds keyed together by the tagged artifact, each its own ADO pipeline with its own trigger, governance and history. Build-once, deploy-many: nothing downstream rebuilds.
