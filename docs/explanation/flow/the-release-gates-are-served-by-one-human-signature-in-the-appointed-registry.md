> **Experimental.** This article is one rule: [FDR-CD-TWORELEASEGATES](../../drs/flow/cd-discipline-and-promotion-flow.md#rule-fdr-cd-tworeleasegates) and [FDR-CD-QUEUE-SIGNATUREISTHEGATE](../../drs/flow/manual-queues.md#rule-fdr-cd-queue-signatureisthegate) merged into one. It holds nothing beyond what those two rules say.

RC and RBC are the two hands-on release gates, and each is a gate, not a pipeline. RC is the gate at main-UAT and RBC the gate at release-uat; each clears when its acceptance testing is verified and a human certifies the environment's occupant. Each is served by one release signature from a user with access, entered in the appointed security registry after the rung's post-deployment test proved the service loads. The signature is one recorded act that this rung passed its check for one named commit; the test is the evidence and the signature is the human's authentication of it, not a second test.
