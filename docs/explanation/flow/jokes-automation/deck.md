# Ten automation jokes that are also true

## Sections

- pete-and-the-timeout: Every slide is a joke about automation, and every joke rests on a record.

## About

The deck is the visual of the automation records told as jokes: the time safeguard, the one loader, the gates, the hooks. Sarah from HR,
John Doe, Livingston Cat, John the computer guy and Pete from IT wander through; the facts do not. Each slide names its record and shows one
diagram of the catalogue, reduced.
