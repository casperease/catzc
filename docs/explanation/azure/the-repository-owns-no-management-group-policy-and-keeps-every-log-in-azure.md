It holds nothing beyond what those two rules say.

Two adoptions are declined for the same reason of scope. Defining policies at the top of the hierarchy is not adopted, because the repository owns no management group and defines no policy of its own; the built-in definitions it assigns exist in every tenant. Keeping raw logs in Azure rather than on premises is not adopted as a choice either, because there is no on-premises monitoring system to forward to, so every log stays in Azure.
