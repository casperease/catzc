# From the research to the architecture

## Eyebrow

Six domains, four roles

## Body

The research is the DORA program held as one record per capability. The thesis frames the product, the principles fix its axioms, the
process is inspected and improved, and design and flow give the platform its shape and its delivery model.

## Tiles

- Research, the DORA records
- Thesis, one source of truth
- Principles, six axioms
- Process, lean flow improved
- Design, the platform's shape
- Flow, the delivery model

## Notes

Only the process domain is optimisable; the axioms are fixed, and no later domain overrides them.
