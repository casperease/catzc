# Every domain explains itself against DORA

## Eyebrow

Closing

## Lead

Each domain holds a DORA-explains page that maps its decisions to the capabilities the research names. Research depends on nothing; every
other domain depends on it.

## Body

The whole chain: one source of truth, six principles that state its mechanics, twelve domains that specialise them, and an external body of
research that validates each step.
